Privacy policy

What stays on your machine

Last updated 20 September 2026. Applies to vspickleball.com and every page on it.

VS Pickleball is a free online pickleball game that runs in your browser. There is no account, no login, and no player database. If you close the tab, the match is gone. A few preferences stay in your browser so you are not resetting the tournament and your shirt color on every visit. This page explains exactly what that means, in plain language.

Stored in your browser

The game writes to localStorage on your device. That data is never sent to us. Right now it includes:

  • Your kit (colors, hat, hair) and player stats.
  • The court you last picked (park, gym, and so on).
  • Tournament progress: which rounds you have already won on this browser.
  • Sound on/off for the current session.

Clearing site data for vspickleball.com in your browser deletes all of it. We cannot restore it because we never had it.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment details. We do not run an account system, a leaderboard, or online multiplayer, so no gameplay data leaves your device. The only measurement we run is the aggregate analytics described below.

Server logs

Like almost every website, our web server and Cloudflare (our CDN and DDoS protection) keep ordinary access logs: IP address, time of request, page or file requested, browser user agent, and referring page. These logs are used to keep the site online, diagnose errors, and block abuse. They are not used to build a marketing profile and are rotated on a normal schedule. Cloudflare’s own handling of this data is described in Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages and game modes people use, so we know what to fix and what to build next. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (_ga, _ga_*) that hold a random identifier, and reports page views, approximate location derived from your IP address (which Google truncates and does not store), device type, and referrer. We do not send names, emails, or anything typed into the game, because there is nothing of the kind to send. Google’s handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can block it with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or any content blocker; the game works exactly the same without it.

Cookies

The game itself does not set tracking cookies beyond the Google Analytics cookies described above. Cloudflare may set a small technical cookie (for example to distinguish real browsers from bots) that contains no personal information. If advertising is enabled (see below), the advertising partner may set cookies as described in that section.

Advertising and Google AdSense

The game is free. To keep it free, the site may display advertising in the columns beside the court through Google AdSense or a similar network. Ads never sit on top of the court or the swing controls. When ads are shown:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of many third-party vendors’ cookies at aboutads.info.
  • How Google uses data when you use our site is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland will be shown a consent message before any personalised ads load, and can change their choice at any time. Non-personalised ads may still use cookies for frequency capping and fraud prevention.

Third-party services

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those files from Google’s servers, which means Google sees the request the way it sees any file download. Google Analytics is loaded as described above. No other third-party scripts are loaded unless advertising is enabled.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on request — everything the game stores is in your own browser and under your control. If you are in the EU/EEA, UK, California, or another region with data-protection rights and believe we hold something about you (for example in server logs), email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The game is a sports toy in a browser and is suitable for general audiences. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent and think a child left data here, clearing the browser’s site data for this origin removes the local kit and tournament save.

Changes to this policy

If the way data is handled changes — analytics, accounts, advertising — this page gets a new date at the top and the change is described here. Continuing to play after that date means the new version applies.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the site in general: info@vspickleball.com.
