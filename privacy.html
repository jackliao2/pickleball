Privacy

What stays on your machine

Last updated 20 September 2026. Applies to vspickleball.com.

There is no account. You do not create a login, and we do not have a player database. If you close the tab, the match is gone. A few preferences stay in your browser so you are not resetting the tournament and your shirt color every visit.

Stored in your browser

The game writes to localStorage on your device. That data does not get sent to us. Right now it includes:

  • Your kit (colors, hat, hair).
  • The court you last picked (park, gym, and so on).
  • Tournament progress: which rounds you have already won on this browser.

Clearing site data in your browser deletes all of that. We cannot restore it. Mute state lives only for the session.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, or payment details. We do not run an account system. As of this writing the game itself does not load a third-party analytics package. The web server and Cloudflare (our CDN and DDoS protection) may keep ordinary server logs — IP address, time, file requested — the way almost every website does. Those logs are for keeping the site up, not for building a marketing profile.

Cookies and ads

The game does not set its own tracking cookies. The layout already leaves columns on the left and right of the court. Those columns are empty placeholders. If advertising is added later, this policy will say so, including any cookies or identifiers the ad network uses. Ads, if they appear, belong in those side columns, not over the paddle.

This policy covers the game at vspickleball.com. If anything here changes, the date at the top will change with it.

Children

The game is a sports toy in a browser. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly store personal information from them. If you are a parent and think a child left data here, clearing the browser’s site data for this origin removes the local kit and tournament save.

Changes

If the way data is handled changes — analytics, accounts, ads — this page gets a new date at the top. Continuing to play after that date means the new version applies.
