About the project

This is pickleball, not tennis with a green ball

A free browser game. No account. No download.

Most “paddle sports” games on the web are tennis. Someone swaps the ball color, shortens the court a little, and calls it pickleball. The kitchen is missing, or it is painted on the ground and does nothing. Serves bounce like a fuzzy yellow ball. You can volley from anywhere.

This project started as a reaction to that. Pickleball has its own geometry and its own bad habits. The court is 20 by 44 feet. The non-volley zone is seven feet. The net is 36 inches at the sideline and 34 at the center. Serves are underhand and diagonal. The return has to bounce. After that you can volley — unless you are standing in the kitchen.

If those rules are optional, the sport collapses into a different game. So they are not optional here.

What you can play

Singles against the computer, doubles with an AI partner, two people on one keyboard, or a six-round tournament that gets harder as it goes. You can change kits, pick an indoor or outdoor court, and tune six stats. The computer on Normal is meant to be beatable if you move people around and stop hitting everything down the middle.

Scoring in this version is rally scoring to 11, win by 2. Whoever wins the rally gets the point. That is easier to follow in a browser than traditional side-out scoring, where only the server scores. The court, the kitchen, and the two-bounce rule still follow USA Pickleball geometry. This site is not affiliated with USA Pickleball, or with any tour, paddle brand, or club.

Who makes it

A small independent project. There is no studio name on the door yet, and no fake company page. The game runs in the browser on purpose: open the page, play. Progress such as tournament wins and your kit stay on your device.

The game lives at vspickleball.com. Bug reports, rule arguments, and anything else: info@vspickleball.com.

Money, later

The game is free. There may be advertising in the side columns later, the same way a lot of free browser games stay online. Ads will not sit on top of the swing button. This page will stay honest about that.
