Pickleball

A real court. A real kitchen. The two-bounce rule. Side-out scoring to 11. This is pickleball, not tennis with a green ball.

Move WASD or mouse · Hold Space / click to swing · Tap for a dink, hold for a drive

How this match is scored

  • Court. 20 × 44 ft, same as official USA Pickleball. The kitchen (non-volley zone) is 7 ft on each side of the net. Net is 36 in at the sidelines and 34 in at the center.
  • Serve. Underhand, from behind the baseline, diagonally into the opposite service box. The serve cannot land in the kitchen or on the kitchen line. Even score = right court, odd = left.
  • Two-bounce rule. The serve must bounce. The return must bounce. After that, volleys are legal.
  • Kitchen. You may stand in the kitchen. You may not volley while in it, on the line, or falling into it. A bounced ball in the kitchen is a dink — that’s the game.
  • Scoring. Traditional side-out: only the server scores. Game to 11, win by 2. A server fault is a side-out, not a point for the receiver.
  • Net cord. A serve that clips the net and still lands in is live (current USA Pickleball: no let serve).

Controls

You (near side): WASD or mouse to move. Space or click to swing — tap a dink, hold a drive, hold + W for a lob.
Player 2: Arrow keys, Enter to swing, ArrowUp while charging to lob.
