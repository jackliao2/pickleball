How this match is scored

  • Court. 20 × 44 ft, same as official USA Pickleball. The kitchen (non-volley zone) is 7 ft on each side of the net. Net is 36 in at the sidelines and 34 in at the center.
  • Serve. Underhand, from behind the baseline, diagonally into the opposite service box. The serve cannot land in the kitchen or on the kitchen line. Even score = right court, odd = left.
  • Two-bounce rule. The serve must bounce. The return must bounce. After that, volleys are legal.
  • Kitchen. Stand just behind the kitchen line after the two-bounce. Tap to dink. Hold to speed-up at the opponent’s body. You may not volley while in the kitchen or on the line.
  • Scoring. Rally scoring: win the rally, get the point. Opponent hits out, nets, or misses — you score. If they were serving, you also take the serve (doubles: first fault is second server, then side-out). Game to 11, win by 2.
  • Doubles. You plus a kitchen partner vs two CPUs. The score is called n – f – server. The first sequence of the game is one serve only (0-0-2); after that both players serve.
  • Tournament. Climb the map, weak to strong. Scout each opponent’s style before you play. Early rounds have fewer stat points. Progress saves on this device.
  • Net cord. A serve that clips the net and still lands in is live (current USA Pickleball: no let serve).

Controls

You (near side): WASD to move and to aim. A/D = left/right landing. W = deeper / walk up to the kitchen. S = shorter. The yellow pip shows the landing.
Kitchen (like the pros): after the two-bounce, walk to the line (W). Soft dinks land in the kitchen — let those bounce, then tap. When a ball sits up above the net, hold Space to yellow for a speed-up. Doubles lives at the kitchen line; singles also comes in, but the four-person hands battle is a doubles thing.
