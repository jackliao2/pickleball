A pickleball game online that plays like the real sport

VS Pickleball is a free online pickleball game that runs in any modern browser — desktop, tablet, or phone — with nothing to install and no account to make. The court is regulation 20×44, the kitchen is a real non-volley zone, the two-bounce rule is enforced, serves are underhand, and rally scoring runs to 11. Pick singles or doubles against the CPU, pass the keyboard for a 2-player match, or build a player and enter the tournament from club rec up to pro.

Four ways to play

  • Singles vs CPU — one on one, three difficulty levels. The easy AI floats dinks and misses drives; hard resets to the kitchen line and punishes anything that pops up.
  • Doubles vs CPU — you and an AI partner against two. Cover your half, poach when the ball floats, and let your partner take the rest.
  • 2 players, same keyboard — a local 2-player pickleball game with WASD on one side and the arrow keys on the other. No login, no lobby, just sit next to each other.
  • Tournament — single elimination. Build a player, scout each opponent, and climb from a Thursday club ladder to a pro final. One loss ends the run.

Real pickleball rules, not arcade rules

Most sports games treat pickleball as tennis on a smaller court. This one keeps the rules that make the sport its own: the serve is underhand and must land past the kitchen, both sides have to let the ball bounce once before anyone volleys, and you cannot volley from inside the non-volley zone. Points are rally scoring to 11, win by 2, so every rally counts whichever side served. If a call ever surprises you, the how to play page walks through each rule with the in-game controls.

Play on phone or desktop

On a keyboard it is WASD to move and Space to dink or drive. On mobile you drag to move and tap to hit, and the camera flattens so the far court stays in view above your thumbs. The whole game is a few hundred kilobytes, loads in a second on a normal connection, and can be added to your home screen so it opens like an app. Nothing is downloaded, nothing is tracked beyond an anonymous page count.

Frequently asked questions

Is VS Pickleball free?
Yes. There is no purchase, no subscription, and no account. Open the page and play.
Does it work on iPhone and Android?
Yes. It runs in Safari and Chrome on any phone from the last few years, with touch controls built for portrait play. Add it to your home screen for a full-screen app feel.
Can I play with a friend?
Yes, on one keyboard. Choose “2 Players · same keyboard” from the menu: one player uses WASD, the other uses the arrow keys. Online multiplayer is not available yet.
How is this different from a tennis game?
The kitchen. You cannot volley from the non-volley zone, the serve is underhand, and the two-bounce rule slows the first exchange down, so points are won at the net with dinks and patience rather than with a big serve.
Do I need to know the rules before I start?
No. The coach panel explains each call as it happens, and the how to play page covers the rest. Most people are dinking at the kitchen line within a couple of games.

How this match is scored

  • Court. 20 × 44 ft, same as official USA Pickleball. The kitchen (non-volley zone) is 7 ft on each side of the net. Net is 36 in at the sidelines and 34 in at the center.
  • Serve. Underhand, from behind the baseline, diagonally into the opposite service box. The serve cannot land in the kitchen or on the kitchen line. Even score = right court, odd = left.
  • Two-bounce rule. The serve must bounce. The return must bounce. After that, volleys are legal.
  • Kitchen. Stand just behind the kitchen line after the two-bounce. Tap to dink. Hold to speed-up at the opponent’s body. You may not volley while in the kitchen or on the line.
  • Scoring. Rally scoring: win the rally, get the point. Opponent hits out, nets, or misses — you score. If they were serving, you also take the serve (doubles: first fault is second server, then side-out). Game to 11, win by 2.
  • Doubles. You plus a kitchen partner vs two CPUs. The score is called n – f – server. The first sequence of the game is one serve only (0-0-2); after that both players serve.
  • Tournament. Climb the map, weak to strong. Early rounds are quick games to 7, later rounds grow to 9, and the championship is to 11. Scout each opponent, clear bonus challenges, and choose upgrades between rounds. Progress and best grades save on this device.
  • Net cord. A serve that clips the net and still lands in is live (current USA Pickleball: no let serve).

Controls

You (near side): WASD to move and to aim. A/D = left/right landing. W = deeper / walk up to the kitchen. S = shorter. The yellow pip shows the landing.
Phone / tablet: drag the left stick to move and aim. Tap SWING for touch; hold it to add pace.
Kitchen (like the pros): after the two-bounce, walk to the line (W). Soft dinks land in the kitchen — let those bounce, then tap. When a ball sits up above the net, hold Space to yellow for a speed-up. Doubles lives at the kitchen line; singles also comes in, but the four-person hands battle is a doubles thing.
