Guides

Pickleball, explained by people who had to code it

Rules, scoring, and court talk in plain English. Every rule here is the same rule the game enforces.

When you build a pickleball game you cannot hand-wave a rule. The kitchen line has to be exactly seven feet, the second bounce has to be checked on the right shot, and the score has to be announced in the right order or players notice in the first rally. These guides are the notes from getting that right, written for someone who has just been invited to a Saturday game and does not want to be the person asking what a dink is.
