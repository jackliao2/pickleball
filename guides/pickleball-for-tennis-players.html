Pickleball for tennis players

Your hands, footwork and court sense transfer. Your serve, your swing and your instinct to hit hard do not. Updated 27 September 2026.

Tennis players usually have a great first hour of pickleball and a frustrating first month. The hand-eye coordination, the footwork and the feel for angles carry over immediately, so rallies from the baseline are easy. Then the ball gets to the kitchen line, the opponents stop hitting hard, and a patient 60-year-old who has never hit a topspin forehand in their life starts winning every point. This guide is about why that happens and how to shorten the frustrating part.

What transfers

  • Hand-eye and timing. You already know how to track a ball and meet it cleanly.
  • Footwork. Split-steps, shuffles and recovery steps work the same way, over shorter distances.
  • Volleys. A compact punch volley is almost exactly right at the kitchen line.
  • Doubles instincts. Moving with a partner, covering the middle, poaching on a weak ball. Pickleball is mostly doubles, so this is valuable.
  • Reading spin. You will read slice and topspin off an opponent’s paddle faster than most beginners.

What does not transfer

The serve

The pickleball serve is underhand: upward arc, contact below the navel, paddle head below the wrist. There is one attempt, not two, and it is not a weapon. Your big serve is gone. The goal is simply deep and in, every time.

Serve and volley

The two-bounce rule says the return must bounce before the serving team hits it. If you serve and charge the net, you will arrive as the return bounces behind you. Serve, stay back, let it bounce.

The big swing

Tennis rewards long, loose, full swings. On a 44-foot court with a solid paddle and a ball that does not compress, a full swing sends the ball long or into the net. Most pickleball strokes are closer to tennis volleys and half-volleys: short, compact, out in front.

Closing the net

In tennis you volley from wherever you like. In pickleball you cannot volley from the seven-foot kitchen, and tennis players fault here constantly, especially from momentum after a volley. Your net position is the kitchen line, not a step inside it.

Hitting through the ball

The deciding shots in pickleball are soft: dinks, drops and resets. A tennis player’s instinct is to hit every ball with intent. At the kitchen line, hitting a low ball hard means lifting it, which sends it up to the opponent’s strike zone.

Scoring

Games to 11, win by 2, and traditionally only the serving side scores, with a three-number call in doubles. It feels strange for a week. The scoring guide explains it.

The five tennis habits that lose the most points

  1. Driving the third shot. Two opponents are at the net; your drive comes back at your feet. Learn the drop.
  2. Speeding up low balls. If the ball is below the net, it has to go up to go over. Dink it and wait.
  3. Backing up for lobs you should let go. On a short court, many lobs go out. And a lob from the baseline is often a gift to the opponent.
  4. Stepping into the kitchen on a volley. Stop your momentum before the line.
  5. Standing at the baseline after the return. Tennis habits keep returners back. In pickleball the returner walks straight to the line.

Equipment differences

The paddle is solid, shorter and lighter than a racket, with no strings; the ball is hard plastic with holes and much slower off the bounce. Tennis players often buy a heavy, elongated paddle because it feels familiar. A mid-weight paddle usually works better while you learn the soft game. The paddle guide explains the trade-offs. The net is lower (34 inches at the centre, 36 at the posts), and the court is under a third the area of a tennis court; see court dimensions and the pickleball vs tennis comparison.

Grip

Most pickleball players use a continental grip for almost everything, the same grip you would use for a tennis volley or serve. It lets you hit forehand and backhand at the line without changing grips. Some players use an eastern forehand for drives, but you will spend most of your time at the kitchen line, where continental is the right default. Hold it looser than you would a racket.

A two-week plan

Week 1: the rules and the line
Play doubles only. Serve deep and in, return deep and walk to the line. Call the score every point. Do not hit a single ball hard at the kitchen line; dink everything below the net. Count how many times you fault in the kitchen and get it to zero.
Week 2: the soft game
Before each session, spend 15 minutes on crosscourt dinks and 15 minutes on third-shot drops from the baseline. In games, drop every third shot for a whole session, even when a drive looks tempting. Then start adding speed-ups, but only on balls above the net.

Most strong tennis players rate around 3.0 to 3.5 in their first month and can reach 4.0 within a season once the soft game clicks. The ratings guide has what each level looks like.

What you gain

Pickleball is easier to find a game for, easier on the body for long sessions, and much more social; open play means showing up and rotating in. Your tennis background makes you a fast learner and a dangerous doubles partner once the kitchen game arrives. And the soft skills you learn will make your tennis volleys better too.

Common questions

Is pickleball easy for tennis players?
The first hour is easy: hitting, footwork and volleys transfer. The soft game at the kitchen line does not, and that is what decides most points above beginner level.
Can I serve overhand in pickleball?
No. The volley serve must be underhand with contact below the navel. The drop serve allows any swing after a bounce, but the ball stays low.
What grip do tennis players use in pickleball?
Continental for most shots, held looser than a tennis grip. It works for both forehand and backhand at the kitchen line.
How long does it take a tennis player to reach 4.0?
Often a season of regular play, once the third-shot drop, dinking and resets become reliable. Most start around 3.0 to 3.5.
Can I use a tennis racket for pickleball?
No. Pickleball paddles are solid with no strings and have strict size limits.
