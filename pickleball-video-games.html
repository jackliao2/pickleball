Pickleball went from a backyard game to America’s fastest-growing sport, and the video games followed a few years behind. There are still only a handful, and they are very different things: a licensed pro-tour sim, a party game with motion controls, VR games that are half workout, and browser games you can open in a tab. PPA Pickleball Tour 2025

The first officially licensed pickleball video game, made by FarSight Studios with the PPA Tour and released in July 2024 on PlayStation, Xbox and PC through Steam, with a Nintendo Switch version shortly after. Its draw is the roster: real pros including Ben Johns and Anna Leigh Waters, with motion-captured movement. You can create your own player and take them through a career of simulated PPA events, or jump into quick play. If you follow the pro tour and want a traditional sports game on a console, this is the one.

Pickleball Smash

Developed by 1PXL Games and published by GameMill Entertainment, Pickleball Smash came out on Nintendo Switch in October 2023. It leans into motion controls, so your swing with a Joy-Con becomes the swing on court, and it supports local multiplayer for up to four people. It is closer to a party sports game than a simulation, which makes it a good pick for a family or a group on one sofa.

Pickleball in VR: Pickleball One, Home Sports and others

VR is where pickleball games feel most physical, because you actually move your arm and feet. Pickleball One on Meta Quest is the most complete of the dedicated VR games, with matches against AI at several difficulty levels, wall and ball-machine drills, a workout mode, mixed reality, and online multiplayer. Home Sports, from Resolution Games, arrived on Quest 3 and 3S in December 2024 as a Wii Sports-style bundle of five sports, pickleball among them, with local and online play. The same studio’s Racket Club (2023) is its own wall-and-racket sport that borrows from tennis, squash, padel and pickleball rather than a pickleball game as such. Smaller Quest titles exist too, but reviews of those are mixed.

Is there pickleball in Wii Sports or Nintendo Switch Sports?

No. The original Wii Sports had tennis but never pickleball, and Nintendo Switch Sports has tennis and badminton but no pickleball. If you want the Wii Sports feel with pickleball, the closest options are Pickleball Smash on Switch, which uses the same swing-the-controller idea, or Home Sports in VR, which openly models itself on Wii Sports.

Which pickleball game is the most realistic simulator?

It depends what you mean by realistic. For real players, real tournaments and TV-style presentation, PPA Pickleball Tour 2025. For the physical feel of the swing and the footwork, VR, because you are actually moving. For the rules and the shape of a point, meaning the soft third shot, the dink battle at the kitchen line, and the fault calls, a rules-first game like VS Pickleball shows you exactly why each point was won or lost.
